Extracted statements

Recognizes Home Depot Pro Xtra, Amazon Business / American Express, Chase, U.S. Bank, Truist, Woodforest and Navy Federal statement layouts. Any other bank falls back to a general parser that works off document structure rather than that bank's labels — a reasonable first attempt, so check the results. If a field can't be found on a given statement, it's left blank in the CSV rather than guessed.

Before you download — who are you?

Asked once, then remembered on this computer. It's recorded with each conversion so we can report on who's using the tool and follow up if something goes wrong. Your statements are still never uploaded — this is just your name.
